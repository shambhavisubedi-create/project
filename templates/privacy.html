Privacy Policy

Last updated: for the GoGather platform, covering account, booking and payment data.

GoGather ("we", "us") runs a travel booking platform for trips within Nepal. This page explains what information we collect from you, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

  • Account details -- full name, username, email address and phone number, when you register.
  • Booking details -- the packages you book, travel dates, traveller counts, and any cancellation or refund requests you submit.
  • Payment information -- we do not store your card or eSewa credentials ourselves. Card payments are processed by Stripe and eSewa payments by eSewa directly; we only keep a transaction reference and the amount paid.
  • Profile photo -- if you choose to upload one, plus a short history of previous photos so you can restore an earlier one.
  • Reviews -- any rating or comment you leave on a completed trip, shown publicly against your name on that package's page.
  • Session data -- a login session cookie so you stay signed in; it identifies your account, not you personally, and is not used for advertising or tracking across other sites.

Why we collect it

  • To create and manage your account, and to let you log back in.
  • To process and manage your bookings, payments, cancellations and refunds.
  • To contact you about a booking's status (currently shown in-app; phone/email notifications are planned but not live yet).
  • To let an admin review and approve bookings, travel groups and refund requests, as a safeguard against fraudulent listings or bookings.

Who can see it

Your booking, payment and contact details are visible only to you and to GoGather admins who manage bookings. A review you leave is shown publicly with your name on the relevant package page. Other travellers in a travel group you join can see your name and messages within that group's chat.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and booking history for as long as your account exists, so you can view your past trips and receipts. If you'd like your account and its data removed, contact an admin to request deletion.

Your choices

  • You can update your profile details, phone number and photo at any time from your Profile page.
  • You can reset your password from the login page if you forget it.
  • You can request cancellation of a booking, or removal of your account, through GoGather support.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text. Every form that changes your data is protected against cross-site request forgery, and login attempts are rate-limited to reduce the risk of a brute-force attack on your account.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be directed to a GoGather admin through the platform.
