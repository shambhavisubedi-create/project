Important information

  • Emergency contacts in Nepal: Nepal Police 100 · Tourist Police 1144 · Ambulance 102 · Fire 101.
  • Carry a printed or digital copy of any booking confirmation and a valid photo ID throughout your trip.
  • Currency is the Nepalese Rupee (NPR) — carry some cash, as cards aren't accepted everywhere outside Kathmandu and Pokhara.
  • Useful Nepali phrases: Namaste (hello), Dhanyabad (thank you), Kasto cha? (how are you?).
  • Timings above are a guide — a guide may adjust the order slightly for weather, traffic, or local conditions.
